Curatorial Notes & Lineup

EDITION 07

Underneath Bengaluru’s November twilight, the Deccan Modular Synth Showcase transforms the historic open-air pavilion of the Indiranagar Club into an experiential sonic laboratory. Seven bespoke Eurorack and semi-modular rigs—handcrafted across South India—converge for an evening spanning deep cinematic drone, generative polyrhythms, and live patch improvisation.

Unlike standard nightclub DJ sets, this showcase centers hardware autonomy: no pre-recorded playback laptops, no safety nets. Each performer builds their audio architecture in real-time, routed through a quadriphonic d&b audiotechnik custom sound array tuned specifically for open rooftop acoustic damping.
